# Moving pages between sections

You can move a page to another section without copying its content by hand. GitBook moves the page out of its current section and adds it to the table of contents of the section you choose.

## Move a page to another section

1. In the table of contents, hover over the page and open its **Actions menu**.
2. Click **Move to…**.
3. Select the destination section.
4. Click **Move**.

The page appears in the destination section’s table of contents, and it’s removed from the original section.

{% hint style="info" %}
To keep the page in both sections, copy it instead. In the page’s **Actions menu**, click **Copy page**, then paste it into the other section from **Add new +** or **Insert +** in the table of contents.
{% endhint %}
